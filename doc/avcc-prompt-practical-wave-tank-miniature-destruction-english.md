# AVCC Prompt — Practical Wave Tank Miniature Destruction

## CORE IDEA

Create a 15-second vertical (9:16) ultra-realistic behind-the-scenes video, casually recorded on an iPhone by a crew member standing at a safe distance from a blockbuster practical-effects film set.

This should feel like real production footage from a large soundstage, not a polished disaster movie shot.

The viewer is watching the making of the effect.

---

## REFERENCE LOCK

`@Image1` defines the exact:

- Architectural appearance
- Building designs
- Facades
- Materials
- Colors
- Proportions
- Overall layout of the handcrafted miniature

Recreate the buildings visible in `@Image1` as a highly detailed physical miniature.

Do not introduce:

- Bukhara landmarks
- Unrelated architecture
- Extra monuments
- Fantasy buildings
- Decorative structures that are not present in the reference image

---

## PRODUCTION SET

The miniature stands inside a huge reinforced practical wave tank on a large soundstage.

Include:

- Blue screens
- Lighting rigs
- Cranes
- High-capacity water pumps
- Thick hoses
- Cables
- Production monitors
- A protected cinema camera in waterproof housing
- Several crew members wearing black “AIVIDEOSKOOL” T-shirts

---

## CAMERA AND SAFETY

The iPhone operator films from a slightly elevated dry observation platform approximately 10–15 meters from the wave tank.

Use a moderately wide shot that keeps visible:

- The entire miniature
- The approaching wave
- Part of the production set

A tall transparent safety barrier and a wide dry working area separate the operator from the tank.

The wave travels laterally across the miniature and away from the operator.

The operator, iPhone, lens, platform, and foreground remain completely dry throughout.

---

## 0–4 SECONDS

One continuous handheld take begins with a slightly distant wide view of the busy soundstage.

The camera slowly pans across crew members making final adjustments to the miniature buildings modeled exactly after `@Image1`.

The cinema camera is secured inside a waterproof housing close to the tank.

An assistant places a clapperboard and quickly retreats to a protected dry walkway.

From off-screen, the director shouts:

> “Rolling... Action!”

The practical-effects supervisor pulls a heavy lever.

Multiple industrial pumps activate simultaneously with a deep mechanical roar.

---

## 4–10 SECONDS

A massive wall of water rapidly rises across the full width of the reinforced tank, significantly taller than the miniature buildings.

The iPhone operator remains stationary behind the distant safety barrier, keeping the entire event in a wide frame.

The enormous practical wave strikes the miniature with overwhelming force.

The first impact instantly tears roofs from the buildings and blasts facades inward.

A fraction of a second later, entire miniature structures break away from their foundations, collapse into large physical sections, and are swept across the tank.

Streets disappear beneath churning water.

Roofs, walls, columns, doors, trees, and large chunks of handcrafted scenery tumble violently in the current.

A powerful second surge follows immediately, sweeping away every remaining structure.

Nothing remains standing.

The complete miniature city is ripped from its base and carried downstream as a dense torrent of water and practical debris.

The director yells:

> “Keep rolling!”

---

## 10–15 SECONDS

The violent current continues dragging the last large fragments of the miniature across the tank.

Smaller debris spins through foaming water while the exposed foundation is completely stripped bare.

By the end, the entire architectural miniature based on `@Image1` has been demolished and washed away.

Only an empty flooded base and scattered fragments remain.

The camera holds the distant wide composition, revealing the massive scale of the practical destruction, blue screens, lighting rigs, pumps, and the devastated wave tank.

Crew members remain safely behind protected barriers.

Water and debris stay entirely inside the reinforced effects zone.

End on the empty miniature foundation as the final surge carries the last recognizable building fragment out of frame.
